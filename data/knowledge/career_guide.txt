Career Guide for Software Developers

INTERVIEWS
Technical interviews typically consist of coding challenges, system design questions, and behavioral questions. 
For coding challenges, practice data structures and algorithms on platforms like LeetCode and HackerRank.
System design interviews test your ability to design scalable systems. Study topics like load balancing, caching, databases, and microservices.
Behavioral interviews use the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Prepare 5-6 stories from your experience.

CV WRITING
A strong developer CV should be one or two pages maximum.
List your most recent experience first. Include the company name, your role, dates, and 3-4 bullet points of achievements.
Quantify achievements where possible. Instead of "improved performance" write "reduced API response time by 40%".
Include a skills section listing your programming languages, frameworks, databases, and tools.
Always tailor your CV to the job description. Mirror the keywords from the job posting.

SKILL GAPS
If you are a backend developer wanting to move into full stack, learn React or Vue.js.
If you lack cloud experience, get an AWS or Azure certification. The AWS Cloud Practitioner is the easiest entry point.
For Python developers, FastAPI is the most in-demand framework in 2026. Django is still widely used for larger applications.
PostgreSQL is the most commonly required database for backend roles. Learn it thoroughly including indexing and query optimization.

SALARY NEGOTIATION
Always negotiate. The first offer is rarely the best offer.
Research market rates on Glassdoor, LinkedIn Salary, and levels.fyi before any negotiation.
Never give a number first. Ask what the budgeted range is for the role.
If the salary is fixed, negotiate other benefits: remote work days, equipment budget, conference allowance, or extra vacation days.

JOB SEARCH STRATEGY
Apply to roles where you meet 70% of the requirements. Do not wait until you meet 100%.
Referrals are 10 times more effective than cold applications. Reach out to your network first.
Follow up on applications after one week with a short polite email.
LinkedIn is the most important platform for developer job search. Keep your profile updated and set yourself to Open to Work.
